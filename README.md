# Alicante in voxels

The port of Alicante in 1 m voxels: the Explanada, the Postiguet, the marina and the Santa Bárbara castle on the Benacantil. You can fly over it, change the date, hour and weather, or walk around.

**Live:** https://raulbr99.github.io/alicante-voxels/ (best on a computer with a recent browser).
